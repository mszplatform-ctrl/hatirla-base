## 1. WHAT XOTIJI IS

XOTIJI is a global AI travel assistant. The name derives from Zazaca for "Kendi Güneşi" (One's Own Sun). Manifesto: **"Toward the Sun / Transfer the Signal / Be Your Own Sun."**

It is not a booking app, not a search engine, not an OTA. It is a **decision engine**: it takes a loose intent ("3 days in Rome, 2 people") and produces a shareable, personalized, monetizable travel plan — hotels, experiences, itinerary, eSIM, insurance, flights — in one flow.

The brand name (XOTIJI) and manifesto ("Toward the Sun") are permanent. They do not change across v1, v2, or v3.

---

## 2. WHO IT'S FOR

Global, English-primary. Not a Turkey-outbound tool. Any traveler, anywhere, who wants a fast, opinionated trip plan instead of twenty open tabs. Language is a delivery detail (TR, EN, ES, DE, AR, PT and beyond), not a scope boundary — the product itself is not localized to one market's travel habits.

---

## 3. THE 3 PERMANENT DIFFERENTIATORS

These are never removed, regardless of what else changes in the roadmap:

1. **Space Selfie — the viral acquisition hook.** Time-travel face-swap across 8 eras × 8 cities, shareable with watermark. This is how strangers find XOTIJI without paid ads.
2. **Trip Toolkit — the single-page experience.** One scroll, one page: itinerary, hotels, activities, eSIM, insurance, flight search, share. Not a multi-step wizard, not a dashboard with ten tabs. The entire trip lives in one place.
3. **6 languages, day one of any given launch.** Not "we'll localize later." Multi-language is a launch requirement, not a v2 feature.

---

## 4. THE LONG-TERM GOAL: LIVING TRIP

Living Trip is the v3 destination — a trip plan that behaves like a living thing instead of a static document. It is explicitly **not** v1 work. Naming it here so scope creep toward it can be caught early and deferred.

Living Trip components (v3, not v1):
- **Constraint Engine** — real visa, budget, and time constraints solved automatically, not just displayed.
- **Group Native** — plans built for multiple travelers with different preferences reconciled into one itinerary, not one user's plan shared read-only.
- **Live Adaptation** — the plan reacts to real-world change (flight delay, weather, closure) without the user re-planning.
- **Transparency** — the user can see why the AI suggested something, not just the output.
- **Travel DNA** — a persistent profile of a user's travel preferences that improves suggestions over time.

---

## 5. WHAT XOTIJI IS NOT (permanent, not just v1)

- Not a full OTA — no complex multi-step booking UI. Affiliate handoff, not in-house booking.
- Not ad-funded. Growth is shareability (Space Selfie) and SEO, not paid acquisition.
- Not a social network. Sharing is one-directional (trip → public link), not a feed or profile system.
- Not infinitely configurable. XOTIJI makes decisions for the user; it is not a filter-everything search tool.

---

## 6. TIMELINE: v1 → v1.5 → v2 → v2.5 → v3

- **v1 (Launch V1, current — see LAUNCH_V1.md):** Global-ready product, real affiliate monetization (GetYourGuide, Booking.com, Airalo, SafetyWing, Kiwi.com), single-page Trip Toolkit, chat-first input, English SEO landing pages, 6 languages, 30 cities, Product Hunt launch.
- **v1.5:** Post-launch data reading, affiliate conversion tuning, city/content expansion driven by real demand, ai_logs + suggestions tables activated for measurement.
- **v2:** Real group native (multiple travelers, reconciled preferences), referral/growth loop, deeper personalization.
- **v2.5:** Constraint Engine groundwork (visa/budget-aware suggestions), Travel DNA profile groundwork.
- **v3:** Living Trip — full Constraint Engine, Live Adaptation, Transparency, mature Travel DNA. The product behaves like a living assistant, not a one-time generator.
